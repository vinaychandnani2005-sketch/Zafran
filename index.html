Our Story

Cooked with Love & Fire

Zafran was born in the narrow lanes of Jaipur's old city, where our founder Ritu Sharma learned to cook from her grandmother. Every recipe here carries a story — of tradition, of rebellion, of home.

We source our spices directly from farmers in Rajasthan, Kerala, and Kashmir. No shortcuts, no compromise. Just honest, bold food that makes you close your eyes with the first bite.
